# The Groove Studio

**Free music tools for Windows, made by musicians for musicians.**
Split songs into parts to learn them, record your takes, program drums, and mix and master, all on your own PC, with no account, no subscription and no uploads.

**[⬇ Download the latest version](../../releases/latest)** · Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) · Free

---

## What's inside

**The Groover: split any song into parts**
Split any recording into vocals, guitar, piano, bass, drums and more, with a tuner and chords on every part. Mute your instrument and play along with the rest of the band, or make karaoke mixes.

**Groover Recorder: record and share takes**
Record your part with your mic or interface, keep your best takes, and send them to your bandmates. The raw take always goes along too.

**Drum Companion for ACID Pro: program beats**
Program a beat, play it live, and export tempo-tagged WAV loops that drop straight onto an ACID timeline.

**Mastering Studio: mix and finish your songs**
Drop in your multitrack stems, shape each one, and run the mix through a master chain and look-ahead limiter. Includes noise reduction, multiband compression and loudness matching for streaming.

## Why it's different

- **Runs on ordinary computers.** No graphics card or high-end PC needed. Splitting runs on your processor and takes roughly 3–6 times the song's length.
- **Private.** Your music never leaves your computer.
- **Simple.** One installer, no setup, no account.

## Installing

1. Download **TheGrooveStudio-Setup-x.x.x.exe** from the [Releases page](../../releases/latest).
2. Double-click it. If Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info → Run anyway**. This appears because the app isn't code-signed (signing certificates are costly for a free app), not because anything is wrong.
3. Open **The Groove Studio** from the Start menu.

**Virus scan:** each release links its [VirusTotal](https://www.virustotal.com) scan in the release notes. One machine-learning scanner (Trapmine) sometimes flags unsigned apps like this one. That's a known false positive; all the major antivirus engines report it clean.

## Supported files

WAV, FLAC, MP3, OGG and AIFF, plus phone recordings (M4A/AAC, MP4, 3GP, Opus, WebM).
